Gold's cardinal sin

Atomic number 79

Gold is a metal, and it is the only thing that comes close to Bitcoin. The reason gold can't be the basis of a global financial system is its inability to travel electronically.

The best you can do is create a piece of paper that says:

"This is worth 1 oz of gold."

Then digitize it and send that instead. Now you have solved one problem and created five that are worse:

  • Who keeps the gold?
  • How much does it cost to store it?
  • Who creates the papers?
  • Are we sure it's real gold?
  • What is the ratio of papers to gold?

We have come down this path already, and I think you know where it leads.

So what do you do with it?

Stop trading it. Stop pricing it in dollars. Stop leveraging it. Save in it, the way you would save in money.
